# Barbados Deep-Sea Biodiversity

This repository contains the code, analysis workflow, and related materials supporting the species ritchnes analysis in the publication "*Assessing the state of deep-sea biological knowledge and charting a path for scientific research in Barbados*".


**Authors**: Kyle Foster<sup>1</sup>, Muriel Rabone<sup>2</sup>, Juliano Palacios-Abrantes<sup>3</sup>, Judith Gobin<sup>1</sup>, La Daana Kada Kanhai<sup>1</sup>, Henri Vallès<sup>4</sup> and Diva Amon<sup>1,5,6</sup>

1. Department of Life Sciences, Faculty of Science and Technology, University of the West Indies St. Augustine,
Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago
2. Natural History Museum, London, United Kingdom
3. Institute for the Oceans and Fisheries, The University of British Columbia, Vancouver, British Columbia,
Canada
4. Department of Biological & Chemical Sciences, University of the West Indies, Bridgetown, Barbados
5. Marine Science Institute, University of California, Santa Barbara, Santa Barbara, CA, United States of
America
6. SpeSeas, D'Abadie, Trinidad and Tobago
